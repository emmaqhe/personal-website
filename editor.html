      How publishing works

      Everything you do here saves automatically in this browser, and every studio or preview window you have open shows the same latest version.

      Visitors see the version in data/site.json on GitHub. To publish your changes:

      1. Click Export. A file called site.json downloads.
      2. On GitHub, open the data folder of your repository, click Add file, then Upload files.
      3. Drop in site.json and click Commit changes. The site updates in a minute or two.

      Shortcuts

      • Ctrl Z undo, Ctrl Shift Z redo
      • Delete remove the selected image from the page (it stays in the library)
      • Ctrl D duplicate, arrow keys nudge, [ ] move back or forward
      • Hold Shift while rotating to snap to 15°
